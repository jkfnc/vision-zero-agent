The question

Where did a person come dangerously close to a moving vehicle or machine?

The answer

A ranked, human-verified incident feed with bounding boxes, plain-English alerts and written reports.

2

How it works

1 · VAST VSS

Ingests and segments video into 5 s clips; YOLO boxes, captions and embeddings in VastDB.

→

2 · Cosmos Reason

Watches each clip (on CoreWeave GPUs) with a street or warehouse safety prompt: risk 0–5, event, machine, people nearby.

→

3 · Checks

Consistency rule (no human near the machine means no alert); human confirm/dismiss with notes.

→

4 · W&B

gpt-oss-120b writes reports and compiles watch rules; Weave traces every call; eval runs measure accuracy.

Web app deployed on the team Kubernetes namespace behind the /app Ingress. Open it from workshop.thecosmoslabs.com → App.

Watch rules in plain English

  • "Alert me when a forklift comes near a worker on the warehouse floor."
  • gpt-oss-120b on W&B inference compiles it to a JSON filter: machine = forklift, warehouse cameras.

Stress test: public-domain archival war film

Explosions: reliable

85% precision, 91% recall across B-17 raids, the RAF attack on the Tirpitz, RAF night bombing, the 1946 Bikini test and a 1945 bulletin.

Bomb release: fooled

10% precision. Parachutes and supply drops read as bombs; a stricter prompt only reached 12%.

Wikimedia Commons, public domain. Sent to Cosmos directly for evaluation; not ingested into the VSS archive.
